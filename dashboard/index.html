cryptographic vouchers

secp256k1 ECDSA, EIP-191 hashed. The same signature settles on Polygon and verifies offline on the merchant phone.

⚡

no bank in the path

UPI Lite needs the issuer bank online. OfflinePay's vouchers are self-verifying, so a kirana store and a delivery partner can transact in a power cut.

card or phone
